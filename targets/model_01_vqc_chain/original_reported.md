# model_01_vqc_chain — reproduction target

This is not a reproduction of a specific published paper's reported numbers -- it is a
faithful reimplementation of the *architecture family* (angle-encoded VQC, alternating
rotation + linear-chain-entangler layers, trained on a parity-labeling task), in the style
of Farhi & Neven (2018, arXiv:1802.06002) and Mitarai et al.'s "quantum circuit learning"
(2018). Used here as the audit battery's first fully-runnable positive case (chain, not
ring, entangler -- see reimplementation.py docstring for why that choice matters to the
matching layer).

**"Reported" accuracy for this session**: whatever `default.qubit` achieves after training
on the full parity dataset (see `sweep_results.md`, generated by re-running
`reimplementation.py`'s circuit through a standard training loop) -- i.e. this file records
the *reference quantum model's own* training result, established once and then held fixed
as the target the MPS surrogate sweep is compared against. There is no external paper
number to match here since this is an architecture-family reimplementation, not a specific
paper reproduction; a specific-paper target model with its own published accuracy number is
Week 3+ follow-up work once the preregistered battery (`PREREGISTRATION.md`) is written.
